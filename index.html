    Bare-Metal C++17 · Apple Silicon / ARM64

    Fused SwiGLU / Transformer FFN Inference Engine

    • Built bare-metal C++17 engine for LLaMA/Mistral FFN with a 5-stage optimization ladder to INT8.
    • Hand-wrote 8-wide ARM NEON microkernel, reaching 37–84x speedup over scalar baseline.
    • Applied INT8 quantization achieving 41.7 GFLOP/s throughput at <0.6% NRMSE error.
    • Fused activation and quantization ops in registers, exploiting P-/E-core scheduling for 1.43–1.48x further gain.
    SwiGLU Microkernel Throughput Ladder
    1. Baseline Scalar C++ (O3) 0.50 GFLOP/s (1.0×)
    2. Auto-Vectorized Clang NEON 18.6 GFLOP/s (37.2×)
    3. Hand-Written 8-Wide NEON Microkernel 36.8 GFLOP/s (73.6×)
    4. Fully Register-Fused NEON + INT8 Quant (Ours) 41.7 GFLOP/s (84.2× Speedup)
