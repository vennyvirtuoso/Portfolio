Beyond Single-Axis Fairness: Learning to Detect Intersectional Biases

Vijendra Kumar Vaishya, Nihar Ranjan Sahoo, Pushpak Bhattacharyya
Center for Indian Language Technology (CFILT), Department of CSE, IIT Bombay
UNDER REVIEW · EACL 2027
Abstract: Current bias mitigation architectures overwhelmingly evaluate demographic disparities across independent, isolated axes. We formulate intersectional bias detection as a high-dimensional retrieval problem over composed stereotype spaces, introducing BiasRetriever. Trained with contrastive objective functions over canonical single-axis anchors, our architecture boosts Jaccard similarity by +18 points over state-of-the-art LLM discriminators across out-of-domain evaluation splits.
1. Introduction & Formulation

Societal stereotypes rarely manifest in isolation; rather, disparities compound across overlapping identity dimensions. While classification heads suffer severe sample scarcity for intersectional cohorts, continuous embedding alignment allows smooth interpolation between canonical demographic axes.

2. Spatio-Temporal Mamba (MIST)

In research accepted at WACV 2027, we tackle temporal medical video segmentation using selective State-Space Models (SSMs). Our Cross-Attention Fusion module (CrossSTF) fuses MinIP spatial priors with multi-scale sequence states, yielding a 0.7801 Dice score on zero-shot cross-dataset evaluation.

3. Clinical Knowledge Graphs (KCDH)

In collaboration with the Public Health Foundation of India (PHFI), we engineered an ontology-aware segmentation pipeline for tobacco cessation across 6,900 clinical concepts with 33k+ edges, achieving 58% Recall@3 and 0.497 MRR across patient diagnostic dialogues.

4. NLTM-Bhashini (Govt. of India)

Architected full-stack sequence tagging models for Marathi Multi-Word Expressions, scaling datasets from 4,260 to 13,900+ active clinical and colloquial annotations (85.05% F1).
