About Me

I am an M.S. by Research scholar in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay), advised by Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharyya at the Center for Indian Language Technology (CFILT).

My academic interests lie at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and High-Performance Systems. Prior to joining IIT Bombay, I completed my Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology from Jabalpur Engineering College.

Research Interests

  • Natural Language Processing: Fairness & bias in language models, evaluation frameworks, retrieval-augmented generation.
  • Sequence Modeling & Vision: State-Space Models (Mamba), temporal representation learning, medical image computing.
  • Systems for Machine Learning: Low-precision quantization (INT8), SIMD vectorization, bare-metal inference optimizations.

Projects & Lab Work

Clinical Conversational Agent for Tobacco Cessation April 2025 – Present
Koita Centre for Digital Health (KCDH), IIT Bombay · Collaboration with Public Health Foundation of India (PHFI)

Working on a clinical decision-support conversational agent utilizing domain-specific ontologies and decision graphs to assess patient readiness and nicotine dependence. Developed ontology-aware text segmentation methods to maintain clinical semantic boundaries.

Speech-to-Speech Machine Translation (SSMT) August 2023 – Present
National Language Translation Mission (NLTM-Bhashini), MeitY, Govt. of India

Developed an annotation platform and sequence labeling models for multi-word expressions in Marathi to facilitate low-resource language corpus curation and machine translation workflows.

Bare-Metal FFN Inference Microkernel Independent Project
C++17, ARM NEON SIMD, INT8 Quantization

Implemented a standalone C++17 feed-forward network inference engine featuring 8-wide ARM NEON vector instructions, register-level operation fusion, and symmetric INT8 quantization.

Gaussian Process & Bayesian Optimization Engine Independent Project
Python, NumPy, SciPy

Built a from-scratch Gaussian Process surrogate model using Cholesky factorizations and log-marginal likelihood maximization, alongside standard acquisition functions for sequential optimization.
